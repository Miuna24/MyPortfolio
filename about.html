About Me

Hi! My name is Jubillee Quizon. I am a graphic designer passionate about creating thoughtful, visually expressive works that evokes emotions while providing truthful and synthetic solutions. My approach combines typography, color, graphics, and composition to build designs that feel intentional and memorable.

I am drawn to designs that tell stories and bring out emotions. Whether I’m creating a visual identity, designing layouts, or conveying a story, I always aim to make every element engaging and connected to the audience.
